# Report analysis

Mode: Operate. Existing Forte concept identity is the visual authority; no replacement world.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A document workbench where financial figures stay connected to their source and can be checked before interpretation.
OWN-WORLD: Burgundy actions, warm white workspace, restrained neutral navigation, compact readable numbers and hairline separators.
STORY: Upload a report, understand what was recognized, inspect or correct obligations, then see the arithmetic change.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left navigation; heading and local-processing indicator; a large upload target plus a short explanation of the output. The populated viewport starts with provisional status, meaningful totals and two complementary analyses.
FORM: Upload-to-workspace composition inherited from the prior report-analysis and scenario concepts. Overview, obligations, simulator, editable review and methodology have dedicated URLs and a shared navigation menu. Mobile navigation scrolls horizontally with visible labels.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Editing an obligation or monthly income recalculates the relevant descriptive totals while preserving unknown values and source labels. No qualitative credit judgement.

## Full-closure simulator extension
Inherit the existing Operate interface. The `/simulator` page shows eligible obligations with native checkboxes, before/after monthly payments, known selected balances and shared income. Desktop keeps selection and comparison adjacent. Narrow viewports show selection first and retain a compact result at the viewport bottom. No selection is pre-filled; no source status changes; no lending grades or approval prediction. Unknown amounts and assumed monthly frequency remain explicit. Amount addition uses integer cents; export preserves source and scenario separately.
